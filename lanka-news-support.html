Common questions

Why don't I see news from a particular website?
Lanka News shows headlines from websites that publish a public news feed. If a site is temporarily unavailable, its stories will return once it is back online. Pull down on the news list to refresh.

How do I save a story?
Tap the bookmark icon next to any story. Your saved stories appear under the Saved button, and they are kept only on your phone.

Who writes the news?
All stories belong to the news websites shown on each story. Tapping a story opens it on that website. Lanka News is not connected to these publishers.

Is my data collected?
No. See our Privacy Policy.
