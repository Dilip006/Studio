Smart DK – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

Smart DK is made by Dilip Kumar Lingarathnam (A2DK Studio), Denmark.

What we use

Location: If you allow it, the app uses your location to find discounted food, fuel prices and stores near you. Your location is sent only to the data providers below to get nearby results. We do not store your location, and it is not linked to you.

Postcode search: If you search by postcode, the postcode is sent to the same providers.

What we do not do

  • No account, no login, no personal profile.
  • No ads, no tracking, no selling of data.
  • We do not collect your name, email or contacts.

Data providers

Results come from public APIs run by Salling Group (food waste, stores, holidays, jobs), OK, Shell and Q8/F24 (fuel prices). Their own privacy policies apply to the requests they receive. Links in the app open the chains' own websites.
