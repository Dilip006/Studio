Lanka News

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026

Lanka News is an app that shows the latest headlines from Sri Lankan news websites in English, Tamil and Sinhala. It is made by Dilip Kumar Lingarathnam (A2DK Studio), Denmark. This policy explains what information the app uses.

The short version

Lanka News does not collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no advertising and no tracking.

Information we do not collect

  • We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or any other personal details.
  • We do not collect your location, contacts, photos or any other data from your device.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

Information stored on your device

Stories you bookmark are saved only on your own iPhone, so you can read them later. This information never leaves your device and is not sent to us. Deleting the app removes it.

How the news is loaded

To show headlines, the app connects directly to the public news feeds of the Sri Lankan news websites it lists, such as Ada Derana, Daily Mirror and Lankadeepa. As with any internet connection, those websites can see basic technical information such as your device's IP address. We do not receive this information.

Reading full stories

When you open a story, it is shown from the news website's own page. That website's own privacy policy applies to what you read there. Lanka News does not control those websites.

Children

Lanka News does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.
